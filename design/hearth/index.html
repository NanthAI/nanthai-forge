Hearth: the locked mark

The Forge F, alive. Six pieces, twelve states, four delight moments, one colour rule. Nothing is added to the letter: its own pieces reach, detach, meet, lie down and come home, and status colour runs through them like a tide. This page is the reference for builders; the shipped set (mark.svg, mark-16.svg, mark.css) sits beside it.

Locked 2026-11-07. Take A "Reach unleashed" for every state. Failed is "Didn't take": the strike makes contact, the join goes red, the arm is pushed back out and rests in line, whole and orange. Rolled back is "Set aside". Every landing throws a spark. Favicon stays the still 16 px cut. No candidates or alternatives remain on this page.

Every animation is CSS, client-side only, and pauses when its mark is off screen or the tab is hidden.

idle

Tap a state to switch; tap it again to replay. Dashed chips play once and hold. "Calm" doubles every looping period, as the product does after 60 s. "Uses" shows how idle grows: one spark on day one, two after 10 landings, three after 100.

The pieces, and the tide rule

  • Stem is main (ink), split below the lower arm.
  • Arms are landed Lanes (brand orange), each an inner part and an outer tip.
  • Crossings warm and teal are where Lanes joined main. Colour enters the F here.
  • Sparks are the only extra thing: at idle, and one thrown by every landing.
  • Nothing else. No bars, dots, gauges, faces or seams. When a state needs a signal, a piece of the F becomes it.

The tide rule.

  1. Rest colours are fixed: ink stem, brand-orange arms, warm and teal crossings. Every state begins and ends there.
  2. A status colour enters the F at a crossing (where a Lane meets main) and flows outward piece by piece, 80 to 120 ms per piece. It never appears on a piece it did not flow to.
  3. While in motion, a status colour may take any piece, including the stem, up to the whole F (landing, the streak, the re-forge).
  4. A still (reduced motion, or the end pose of a one-shot) holds status colour on at most the crossing plus the one arm that changed. The stem is never a solid status colour in a still. Failed holds red on the lower crossing only: the arm stays orange because the work is intact. Landed's still is the whole approved F with the crossing and the lower arm green, so the only state whose shape equals idle is the one that means "back to normal".
  5. Orange never means a status, and no status colour is decoration: a colour change always comes with a shape change on the same piece (reach, orbit, lift, flag, push-out, set-aside, lie-down), so a colour-blind user reads every state from the shape and the glyph beside it.
  6. Status colours keep their meaning: blue working or checking, amber overlap or waiting, green landed, red failed, grey rolled back.

The strike is the house style. Every one-shot is built from three beats, and every looping state is the first beat stretched out.

1 Anticipation, 120 msThe piece pulls away from where it is going: the arm backs 6 px out of the stem.
2 Impact, 100 msThe piece slams home. The crossing flashes for 100 ms, the stem rings (scaleX 1.14 for 500 ms), and the status colour enters at the crossing. When the landing does not take, this beat is replaced by contact: the join goes red, no flash, no ring.
3 Settle, 400 to 600 msThe piece comes to rest with a strong ease-out, no bounce. Colour floods outward piece by piece, holds, and later drains back to rest colours.

Every state at 512, 64, 32 and 16 px

The 512 geometry is shown at 128 px here so the page fits a phone; opens every card to full size. The 32 px cut is the 64 geometry scaled. The 16 px cut is a separate pixel-aligned drawing (stem 3 px, arms 3 px, crossings 3 × 3, the spark 1 px); every move is a whole-pixel step with stepped timing. Tap a row to replay it.

Reduced-motion stills

The base rule of each state is its end pose; animation only adds movement. With motion reduced every state is still and every change is instant. Every still differs from idle in shape, not only in colour, except landed, by design (tide rule 4). The glyph and word beside the mark always carry the state too: the product never relies on the mark alone.

Terminal: nforge frames per state


  

  

  
Frames are plain Unicode (box drawing and block elements), cycled by nforge at 550 ms only on a TTY; one-shots run their frames once and hold the still. The idle spark is a middle dot walking along the bar once per cycle. Waiting lifts the tip (▀ ↔ ▔); checks walks it round (╹ ╻). Colour is ANSI where supported; the glyph and word always follow the frame. Logs, JSON and piped output get the ASCII still form.






  
Story players

  

    
idle

    

    
"A Lane's life" runs idle → working → many → checks → overlap → waiting → queued → landing → landed → idle with real transitions. "Then it fails" cuts the landing with a failure, then rolls back and sets the work aside. "A busy day" lands three Lanes together, then a streak of five.

  

  

    
States are poses. Every persistent state is a set of transforms and fills on the same six pieces, with a 320 ms transition on transform and 240 ms on colour. Switching state tweens from wherever each piece is.

    
One-shots can be interrupted. Landing, landed, failed and rolled back have a start and an end. A state change removes the animation and the pieces tween to the new pose; the largest visible jump is the lower arm's 6 px pull-back if landing is cut in its first 120 ms.

    
What loops and what never loops. Idle, working, checks, overlap, waiting and queued loop (slowest 8 s, fastest 2.4 s). Landing, landed, failed and rolled back play once and hold. Paused has no motion. Delight moments play once per event and are never queued or repeated.

    
Calming down. After 60 s in a looping state every period doubles (one multiplier). After 5 min the still pose holds and the glyph beside the mark carries the state alone. A state change resets the clock.

Delight that grows with use

  

  

    
Thresholds. Idle shows a second spark after 10 landings and a third after 100. The streak plays at 5 clean landings in a row. "Many" plays when 3 or more Lanes land in one batch. The re-forge plays once, at the 1,000th landing. The first-landing assembly plays once per project.

    
Counters are per project and local (browser storage; no server call). Each moment plays once per event; a user who lands 50 times a day sees the strike 50 times and the re-forge once in their life. Nothing on this list loops, and nothing is always on.

  






  
Cost

  
Client-side only. One inline SVG and one CSS file; no JavaScript per frame; no requests; 0 server calls at idle and at every state change (state comes from counts the top bar already has; delight counters are local). Paused when the tab is hidden or the mark is off screen. Favicon is the still 16 px cut and never follows state.

  

    
Budget for builders. Shipped CSS ≤ 24 KB uncompressed (≤ 6 KB gzipped), SVG ≤ 1 KB each, 0 extra requests, 0 Convex calls, no per-frame script, no timers while idle (the CLI ticker runs only on a TTY and only while a state is live).
