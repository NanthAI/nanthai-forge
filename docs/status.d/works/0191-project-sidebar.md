- The signed-in project sidebar nests pages under the active place, including Plan's built-in and browser-saved views. Collapse it with the footer button or `[`. Hover or focus a collapsed icon for its pages, or tap it on a touch screen. The browser remembers the wide and narrow choices locally. On phones, the page title opens the current place's pages, and More opens Stats and the Settings pages. The same addresses work as links and in browser history.
