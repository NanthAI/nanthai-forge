- New hosted solo projects start Trusted and can land an ordinary change without a
  check. The landing retains its exact source, its receipt and a revert command.
  The owner still approves protected paths. After another member joins, Landing
  and checks offers the owner earned trust once. Existing projects retain their
  policy. The [Quickstart](../../quickstart.md) takes a new project from hosting
  to its first landing and puts checks and approval settings later in the guide.
