A device can be limited to folders of one project. Saves outside them and
reverts stop with `AUTH_SCOPE_DENIED`, and so do landings whose own files lie
outside them, before any check runs. The grant ends 7 days after its last write
or 30 days after it was given (`AUTH_EXPIRED`), renewing at most hourly on a
write, with no extra read or idle call. A landing from a device signed out,
removed or past its grant stops at publication (`AUTH_EXPIRED`, one credential
read, grant landings only). Landings say whether an agent or a person built
them, and History and `landings {kind}` filter by it. The GitHub device check
keeps the typed code through its return and is offered only to an account with
linked GitHub.
