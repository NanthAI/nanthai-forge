An owner links their own teams to a project with read or write. Every current
member gets that role, the highest of their direct grant and every linked team,
and leaving the team ends it on the next call. The board, agent coordination and
Lane holders belong to the project's team on every project. With `boardAudience`
set to `team-and-contributors`, the project's readers may also message the team;
no setting opens them to everyone. People and access shows the linked teams to the owner, who links, changes or unlinks one there.
