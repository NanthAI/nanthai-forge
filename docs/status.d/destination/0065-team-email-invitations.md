Team invitations by email are always pending until accepted. Team owners see the typed address as pending, the same whether or not an account uses it, and can cancel it. The person with the current verified address accepts or declines on Home. Team invitations use the shared inviter counter and the existing account block preference. A team add by Forge handle is pending too, until that account accepts, declines or blocks on Home. See [team invitations](../../access.md#team-invitations).
