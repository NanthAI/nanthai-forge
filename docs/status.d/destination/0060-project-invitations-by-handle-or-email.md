Project invitations by handle or email stay pending until the invited person accepts on Home or `/account/people`. **Use another account** opens that page for the next account after sign-in. Invitations expire after seven days and leave the inbox. Recipients can decline or block an inviter across projects. Removing a member or lowering them to read ends their earlier invitations. **People and access** shows invitation and email status. Invitation email is off by default. With email off, an owner can copy and share the invitation link. See [project access](../../access.md#invitations).
