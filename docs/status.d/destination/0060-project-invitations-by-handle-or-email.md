Project invitations by handle or email stay pending until the invited person accepts on Home or `/account/people`. **Use another account** opens that page for the next account after sign-in. Invitations expire after seven days and leave the inbox; recipients can decline or block an inviter across projects. Removing a member or lowering them to read ends their earlier invitations. People and access shows invitation states and mail failures across pages. Owners can also page through pending invitations in the older Access settings. Legacy add APIs explain ended invitations. Handle and email invitations share the approved inviter limits, including while email is off. Invitation email is independently off by default, requires a verified sender, and must remain off until the enablement requirements on [invitation email Issue c6629fda](https://dev.forge.nanthai.tech/?project=mh7aafndrxmj8c2q0931zbjk2n8h4ffs&projectTab=planning&planningItem=c6629fda-c29f-48e9-b677-5094e5cf0d9b) are complete. See [project access](../../access.md#invitations).
