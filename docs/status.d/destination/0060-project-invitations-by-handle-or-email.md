Project invitations by handle or email stay pending until the invited person accepts on Home or `/-/account/people`. They expire after seven days and leave the inbox; recipients can decline or block an inviter across projects. Removing a member or lowering them to read ends their earlier invitations. People and access pages invitation states and mail failures; legacy add APIs explain ended invitations. Handle and email invitations share the approved inviter limits, including while email is off. Invitation email is independently off by default, requires a verified sender, and must remain off until the enablement requirements on [invitation email Issue c6629fda](https://nanthai-forge-dev.netlify.app/?project=mh7aafndrxmj8c2q0931zbjk2n8h4ffs&projectTab=planning&planningItem=c6629fda-c29f-48e9-b677-5094e5cf0d9b) are complete. See [project access](../../access.md#invitations).
