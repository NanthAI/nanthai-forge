Lanes › Who is touching what draws who waits on whom as chains under the pairs: each Lane a short chip with
its state dot, an arrow to the Lane, person or main it waits on with the wait's time on the arrow, longest
wait first, a loop marked, and a phone stacking each chain downwards. A long queue keeps its two ends and
says how many Lanes it skips. The landing queue and who has what are not repeated there; Lanes › Open and
the top flow strip hold them. When the overlap read is partial, the page says how many Lanes were compared
and why a pair may be missing. A dock with one region (Lanes › Open) draws no header bar or drag handle.
The top bar's name beside the mark is always Forge; a conflict or failed check is announced, not drawn there.
